# Focus
- study dates
- SUV values and findings exactly as reported
- staging as recorded in the report
- comparison with other imaging as documented

# What to look for in the records
- The tracer, dose, uptake time and blood glucose when the report states them, and the date of the study.
- Each site of uptake with its SUVmax exactly as written, and whether the report calls a finding physiological or indeterminate.
- The stage the report itself records, copied exactly; never derived from the findings.
- Whether the report compares with the CT or MRI in the Case File and whether the two agree, as documented.

# Points for review to raise
- A PET/CT and a CT of similar dates that report different nodal or distant findings.
- An indeterminate finding for which the report suggests a further study that is not in the Case File.
- A PET/CT report that is preliminary or lacks a signature.
