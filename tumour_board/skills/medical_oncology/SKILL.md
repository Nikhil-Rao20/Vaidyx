# Focus
- clinical history and presenting complaint as documented
- performance status as written
- comorbidities and current medications as documented
- prior and ongoing treatment as documented
- laboratory values relevant to fitness as reported
- the question the treating team asks the board

# What to look for in the records
- The ECOG or Karnofsky status, who recorded it and on which date; whether a later note records a different one.
- Comorbidities and medications listed in the notes, including anticoagulants, diabetes and renal or liver disease as written.
- Treatment already given: regimen, cycles, dates and completion, exactly as the treatment summary or discharge summary states; toxicity as documented.
- The latest laboratory values the protocol lists as baseline (blood counts, renal and liver function, tumour markers), with the report dates, and whether any is older than the other baseline records.
- The clinical question for the board as written in the referral or note; whether it is missing.
- Patient preference or a documented goals-of-care discussion, when present in the notes.

# Points for review to raise
- Two notes that record different performance status or different treatment histories.
- A baseline laboratory report that predates a treatment cycle documented later.
- A referral that states no question for the board.
