# Focus
- documented tumour location and extent from reports
- prior procedures and endoscopy as documented
- comorbidities and performance status as documented
- previous Tumour Board decisions and whether their actions are documented as done

# What to look for in the records
- The site, size and extent of the tumour as each report words it (distance from the anal verge, relation to the mesorectal fascia, chest wall or vessel contact), copied exactly.
- Prior biopsies, endoscopies and operations with their dates and findings as written; the operative note of any earlier surgery.
- Anaesthesia or fitness assessments present in the notes, and the performance status as documented.
- Whether an earlier Tumour Board record lists actions, and whether a later document shows each action done.

# Points for review to raise
- Two reports that place or size the tumour differently.
- A procedure mentioned in a note whose report is not in the Case File.
- An earlier board action with no document showing it was carried out.
