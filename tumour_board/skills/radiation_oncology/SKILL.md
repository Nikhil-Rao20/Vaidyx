# Focus
- radiotherapy delivered as documented: dose, fractions, dates, concurrent agents, toxicity
- restaging after treatment as documented
- previous Tumour Board plans involving radiotherapy and whether follow-up is documented

# What to look for in the records
- The total dose, number of fractions, start and end dates, technique and concurrent systemic agent exactly as the treatment summary records them.
- Toxicity or treatment interruptions as documented, with dates.
- A restaging study after the radiotherapy, its date relative to the end of treatment, and whether its report is final.
- Earlier board decisions that planned radiotherapy and whether a treatment summary shows it delivered.

# Points for review to raise
- A treatment summary and a clinical note that record different doses or dates.
- A restaging report dated before the end of treatment.
- A planned radiotherapy course with no treatment summary in the Case File.
