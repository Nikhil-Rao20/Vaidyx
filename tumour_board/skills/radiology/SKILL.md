# Focus
- modality and date of each study
- lesion sizes and measurements exactly as reported
- nodal and distant findings as reported
- comparison with prior imaging as documented
- imaging that is missing, pending or not final
- differences between reports

# What to look for in the records
- The study date and the report date of every imaging report, and whether the report is final and signed.
- Whether each report states the technique (contrast, phase, region covered) and compares with a prior study, as the reporting checklist asks.
- The same measurement given differently in two reports of the same study, or a size that changed between studies without the report saying so.
- Imaging the protocol lists for this cancer that has no report in the Case File, or only a draft or preliminary one.
- A BI-RADS, Lung-RADS or other category written in the report; copy it exactly, never assign one.

# Points for review to raise
- A report whose impression and body disagree, or two reports of the same study with different findings.
- A restaging study that predates the treatment it was meant to follow.
- A pending addendum, a report awaiting comparison films, or a study mentioned in a clinical note that is not in the Case File.
