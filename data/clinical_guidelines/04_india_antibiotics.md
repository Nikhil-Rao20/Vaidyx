# Antibiotic Guide — India (Indian Resistance Patterns)
# Source: ICMR AMR Surveillance Network, INSAR, API, IDSA adapted for Indian context

---

## KEY INDIA RESISTANCE PATTERNS (Critical Context)

### Community-Acquired (ICMR AMRSN 2022)
- **E. coli (UTI):** Fluoroquinolone resistance ~60–80%; Co-trimoxazole ~60%; Ampicillin ~80%; ESBL producers ~40–60% in community
- **Klebsiella:** ESBL >50% community; Carbapenem-resistant (CRK) rising in hospitals (~15–20% tertiary)
- **S. typhi:** Fluoroquinolone resistance >90%; XDR strains in 5–10% in some cities
- **S. pneumoniae:** Penicillin resistance ~5–10% in India (lower than Western countries)
- **S. aureus:** Community MRSA ~15–20%; Hospital MRSA ~25–40%
- **Acinetobacter (ICU):** Carbapenem resistance >60% at many Indian tertiary centres
- **Pseudomonas (ICU):** Carbapenem resistance ~30–40%
- **N. gonorrhoeae:** Ciprofloxacin resistance >90%; Azithromycin resistance rising

### Hospital-Acquired (HAI in India)
- CRE (Carbapenem-Resistant Enterobacteriaceae) — rising threat; treat with Colistin/Polymyxin B + Meropenem combination or Ceftazidime-Avibactam
- CRKP (Carbapenem-Resistant Klebsiella) — OXA-48, NDM-1 type (India a reservoir for NDM producers)

## EMPIRIC ANTIBIOTIC PROTOCOLS — INDIA

### Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP) — Outpatient
- **Mild:** Amoxicillin (Mox, Novamox) 500 mg TDS × 5–7 days OR Azithromycin (Azee) 500 mg OD × 5 days (if atypical suspected)
- **Moderate:** Amoxicillin-Clavulanate (Augmentin, Clavam) 625 mg TDS × 7 days + Azithromycin 500 mg OD × 5 days
- **AVOID Ciprofloxacin as monotherapy for CAP** — fluoroquinolone resistance high; use only if beta-lactam allergy + culture sensitivity confirmed

### CAP — Hospitalised (Non-ICU)
- **Piperacillin-Tazobactam (Tazact, Zosyn)** 4.5 g IV q6h + **Azithromycin (Azithral IV)** 500 mg IV OD × 5 days
- OR **Ceftriaxone (Monocef, Rocephin)** 2 g IV OD + Azithromycin 500 mg IV OD × 5–7 days

### CAP — Severe (ICU)
- **Meropenem (Meromer, Meropenem IP)** 1–2 g IV q8h + **Azithromycin** 500 mg IV OD
- If MRSA suspected (post-influenza, cavitating pneumonia): Add **Vancomycin** 15–20 mg/kg IV q8–12h OR **Linezolid (Lizolid, Zyvox)** 600 mg BD IV/PO
- If Legionella suspected: Levofloxacin (Tavanic) 500 mg IV OD (check local susceptibility)

### Healthcare-Associated Pneumonia (HCAP)/Hospital-Acquired Pneumonia (HAP)
- **Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h + Amikacin (Amicin) 15 mg/kg IV OD**
- If Acinetobacter/Carbapenem-resistant: **Colistin (Colistin sulphomethate sodium)** 2.5 mg/kg q12h IV + Meropenem 2 g q8h (extended infusion over 3h) + Sulbactam
- Duration: 7 days for HAP, 8–15 days for VAP

### Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) — India

**Uncomplicated UTI (women):**
- **Nitrofurantoin (Nitrofurantoin IP, Macrobid)** 100 mg MR BD × 5 days — FIRST-LINE (low resistance in India)
- **Fosfomycin (Fosfocin)** 3 g PO single dose — excellent for ESBL E. coli (>90% susceptibility in India)
- **Co-trimoxazole (Bactrim, Septran)** 960 mg BD × 3 days — use only if local susceptibility >80% (check ICMR data)
- **AVOID fluoroquinolones as first-line UTI** — preserve for complicated/resistant cases

**Complicated UTI/Pyelonephritis:**
- Amoxicillin-Clavulanate 1.2 g IV q8h OR Ceftriaxone 2 g IV OD × 10–14 days
- ESBL confirmed: **Ertapenem (Invanz)** 1 g IV OD × 10 days OR **Meropenem** 500 mg IV q8h (reserve carbapenem)
- Culture-guided after susceptibility

### Skin & Soft Tissue Infections (SSTI)

**Non-purulent cellulitis:**
- **Cloxacillin (Cloxapen, Bioclox)** 500 mg QID × 7 days — MSSA coverage
- **Amoxicillin-Clavulanate** 625 mg TDS — broader; India standard

**Purulent SSTI/Abscess (MRSA risk):**
- Incision & drainage (primary treatment)
- Add **Co-trimoxazole 960 mg BD** (community MRSA — check local data) OR **Doxycycline 100 mg BD** × 5–7 days
- Severe: **Vancomycin 25 mg/kg IV loading → guided by AUC/MIC** OR **Linezolid 600 mg BD**

**Necrotising Fasciitis:**
- **Meropenem 2 g IV q8h + Vancomycin 25 mg/kg IV + Clindamycin (Clindac, Dalacin) 900 mg IV q8h** (toxin suppression)
- Urgent surgical debridement — DEFINITIVE treatment
- Add anti-fungals if immunocompromised

### Sepsis — Empiric India Protocol

**Community-acquired source unknown:**
- **Ceftriaxone 2 g IV OD + Metronidazole (Flagyl, Metrogyl) 500 mg IV TDS** — abdominal source
- **Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h** — broader cover; India first choice for severe sepsis

**Hospital-acquired/ICU sepsis (de-escalate after cultures):**
- **Meropenem 2 g IV q8h** (extended 3h infusion if ESBL/KPC) + **Vancomycin 25 mg/kg loading IV**
- Neutropenic fever: Add **Micafungin (Funguard) 100 mg IV OD** empirically after 96h of persistent fever

**CRE/NDM-producing organisms (last resort India):**
- **Colistimethate Sodium (CMS, Colistin)** 9 million units IV loading → 4.5 million units q12h (renally adjusted) — Polymyxin-based; nephrotoxic
- **Ceftazidime-Avibactam (Avycaz, Forticef-A)** — NOW AVAILABLE IN INDIA; for KPC and OXA-48; 2.5 g IV q8h (3h infusion); preferred over colistin if available
- **Tigecycline (Tigecyl, Tygacil)** 100 mg IV loading → 50 mg q12h — bacteriostatic; poor for bacteraemia (low serum levels); use for ABSSSI, IAI only

### Tuberculosis — NTEP (National TB Elimination Programme) Protocols India

**Drug-Sensitive TB (DS-TB) — Standard 6-Month Regimen:**
- **2HRZE / 4HR** (H=Isoniazid, R=Rifampicin, Z=Pyrazinamide, E=Ethambutol)
- Intensive phase (2 months): HRZE daily
- Continuation phase (4 months): HR daily
- **Fixed-dose combinations (FDC tablets)** — NTEP provides free under Nikshay Poshan Yojana

**DOTS (Directly Observed Treatment Short-course)** — mandatory under NTEP

**Drug Weights and Doses:**
- Isoniazid (INH, H): 5 mg/kg (max 300 mg/day)
- Rifampicin (R): 10 mg/kg (max 600 mg/day)
- Pyrazinamide (Z): 25 mg/kg (max 2 g/day)
- Ethambutol (E): 15 mg/kg (max 1.2 g/day)

**MDR-TB (Multi-Drug Resistant):**
- Bedaquiline (Sirturo) + Pretomanid + Linezolid (BPaL) — 6-month regimen (NTEP adopted 2022)
- Longer regimen: Bedaquiline + Clofazimine + Linezolid + Cycloserine (based on DST)
- All MDR-TB managed under NTEP; free drugs provided
- Mandatory notification to NTEP and district TB officer

**Preventive Therapy (TPT):**
- **Isoniazid 5 mg/kg (max 300 mg) OD × 6 months** for TB contacts, PLHIV
- Alternate: **3HP** (Isoniazid 900 mg + Rifapentine 900 mg weekly × 12 doses) — newly introduced

### Source: NTEP (2022 NTEP Guidelines), RNTCP, MoHFW India

### Meningitis — India

**Community Bacterial Meningitis:**
- **Ceftriaxone 2 g IV q12h** (adults) — FIRST-LINE for S. pneumoniae, N. meningitidis, H. influenzae
- **Dexamethasone (Dexona)** 0.15 mg/kg IV q6h × 4 days — START before or with first antibiotic dose (proven benefit for S. pneumoniae and H. influenzae in India)
- Paediatric: Ceftriaxone 100 mg/kg/day divided q12h + Dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg q6h × 2–4 days

**TB Meningitis:**
- 2HRZS / 10HRE (Streptomycin in intensive phase)
- **Dexamethasone 0.4 mg/kg/day** IV × 4 weeks → taper over 4 weeks — ALL TBM regardless of stage (Lancet RCT evidence)
- Duration: 12 months total (2 months intensive + 10 months continuation)

**Cryptococcal Meningitis (HIV/Immunocompromised):**
- **Amphotericin B deoxycholate (AmB-D, Fungizone)** 0.7–1 mg/kg IV OD × 2 weeks + Flucytosine 25 mg/kg QID (if available)
- Then Fluconazole (Forcan, Zocon) 400 mg OD × 8 weeks → 200 mg OD maintenance
- Note: Liposomal AmB (AmBisome) preferred if renal impairment; widely available in Indian tertiary centres
