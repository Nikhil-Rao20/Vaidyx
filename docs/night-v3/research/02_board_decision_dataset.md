# Board decision record: what to capture (research for Phase 3)

Research for plan item 0.3, feeding items 3.1 and 3.2 (decision record v2, decision D7). Written 27/10/2026. Read-only on the code; nothing was run.
Page rule: "PDF p." is the PDF page index plus 1 (what a reader shows); "printed p." is the number printed on the page where it differs.
Cached text: `/data/vaidyx/night-v3/research_text/` (NHS, Victoria, PM-JAY, CRUK, Greater Manchester).

## 1. Summary

1. Every source wants the same core: the recommendation itself, the stage used, who disagreed (by name) when there was no consensus, what must happen next (investigations, referrals), whether a trial was considered, and who tells the patient.
2. NHS England 2020 lists ten "minimum core data" items. Only the last (the MDT recommendation and treatment pathway) is the board's output; the rest are inputs the board must have accounted for (stage, fitness, comorbidity, trial, genomics, patient preference).
3. Victoria 2018 is the most precise on the output. Its "Live data entry, part 2" has: stage, report errors or changes, treatment recommendation, divergent recommendations, the name of each clinician who disagreed, recommended referrals (by role and service), trial suitable (yes, no, unknown) and trial name.
4. Victoria and Greater Manchester add communication timing: treating team within 24 hours (Victoria) or 1 working day (GM), GP within a week (Victoria), patient within 2 working days where possible (GM). GM adds that the person who tells the patient must be named in the outcome.
5. CRUK 2017 adds three things: enter the record live and show it to the room, record the reason when a case is not discussed because information was missing, and use a proforma. NHS 2020 and GM both followed it.
6. PM-JAY HBP 2.2 does not define a decision form. It says a multidisciplinary tumour board should decide cancer care (or the nearest Regional Cancer Centre when none exists), pre-authorization is needed "at each step", and "a detailed Oncology Treatment Plan" matters. So the record needs only a clear yes, no or not applicable for plan approval and a link to the plan.
7. The NCG Virtual Tumour Board case template has no recommendation fields. Its last slide is only "Questions for the tumor board" (slide 6). It is an input form, so nothing there to copy for the output.
8. ACS Commission on Cancer standard 2.5 is the cancer conference standard ("Multidisciplinary Cancer Case Conference"). I could not read the official text (the host refused or timed out); what it requires per case, from secondary sources, is stage, treatment planning against evidence-based guidelines, and where relevant genetic testing, research studies and supportive care. Treat as unverified until a clinician reads the standard.
9. ESMO molecular tumour boards (Westphalen 2025) matter only for a future molecular board. Its report holds treatment recommendations, assessment of possible germline findings, and advice on further testing. Not needed for the general board record now.
10. India adds: PM-JAY approval, Indian IDs and names, and the owner's own wish for the head-absent agents. No public Indian hospital MDT policy or NCG VTB rule text on the outcome was found (see section 7).

### 3.3 Mismatches found

1. Outcome labels differ three ways. `postboard.OUTCOMES` has four (no "noted"); "not_discussed" reads "Not discussed: information missing" there but "Not discussed: moved to the next meeting" in `meetings.OUTCOMES` (line 31). The UI says "Not discussed" and the hint "Moves to the next meeting". The minutes use the postboard label, so a case moved to the next meeting is minuted as "information missing".
2. "Noted" is stored as `outcome_type` "decision" (meetings.py line 1127). So a noted case raises the five communication info flags and is counted as a decision in metrics and `his_case.py`.
3. Limits differ: rationale 2000 UI vs 4000; intent 200 vs 300; next step 300 vs 2000; referred to 200 vs 300; concern text 1000 vs 2000. The old route and postboard have no cap on `decision_text`.
4. Intent: the form's free text rarely matches curative, palliative or not_stated, so `intent_as_recorded` stays empty and the flag "Intention to treat not stated" is raised even when the board stated it. Minutes show only `intent_as_recorded`.
5. Six fields are accepted but never asked, so every decision raises info flags (`patient_informed_date`, `key_worker`, `trial_considered`, `intent_as_recorded`, `consent_recorded`), plus the review flags "Decision record is missing: actions" (breast.json `post_board.required_record_fields` is decision_text, recorded_by, participants, actions) whenever no action was made.
6. `next_review`, `report_corrections_raised` exist only on the old board route.
7. The Case File panel (BoardFormPanel) shows the form fields only; minutes and deck show different subsets (minutes lack rationale, responsible doctor, next step; deck lacks key worker and trial).
8. The NCG VTB export (`vtb_export.py`) carries the Case File summary only, no board record.
9. No general edit of a saved record exists. Versions are created only when a flag is resolved and the decision text is amended (`_new_version`: version, by, reason, change). Communication edits make no new version, but a history list.
10. breast.json `record_fields_source` cites EUSOMA 2020, Victoria 2018 and PM-JAY s.3.9; the other protocols were not checked here.

## 5. The proposed form

Six steps, 36 fields. All present fields stay (marked "keep"). The coordinator types or dictates (dictation fills editable text boxes only). The target is two minutes: to save, the outcome is required, and for a decision also the decision text, `intent` (one choice; "not stated" is allowed) and `responsible_doctor` (pre-filled with the Case File's owner), as rule 2 below says, so saving stays fast; step 1 and 2 happen in the meeting, steps 3 to 6 are mostly pre-filled or ticks, and the rest can be left for after the meeting (marked "after"). Everything the owner's consultant may correct afterwards is marked "C".

Kinds: choice, multi (several choices), short, long, date, person, dept, yn (yes, no, not applicable or not stated).

## 6. Rules the backend should enforce

1. One outcome label table, in one module, used by postboard, meetings, minutes, deck and UI (decision, deferred, referred, not_discussed, noted). Store `outcome_type` equal to the real outcome, including "noted". A noted case raises no communication flags and is not counted as a decision.
2. Required by outcome: decision needs `decision_text` (at least 5 characters) and `intent` (value or "not stated") and `responsible_doctor`; deferred needs `awaited` and `awaited_from`; referred needs `referred_to`; not_discussed needs `not_discussed_reason`; noted needs nothing. Everything else is optional and may be filled later.
3. Limits in one table in the backend, sent to the UI (a `limits` endpoint or a shared constant) so the two cannot drift: decision 4000, rationale 4000, long fields 2000, short fields 300, names 120. Reject over limit with a plain message; do not cut silently.
4. `intent` is a choice. Map the old `intent_as_stated` text to `intent` only when it matches a value; keep the free text as given.
5. Each investigation row becomes an action for its department with a due date; each referral creates a request to its department. A row without a department gets the existing "no Responsible Department" flag. The "missing actions" review flag fires only when the outcome is decision and there is no investigation, referral, next step or awaited item (not for every decision).
6. The information flags (patient informed date, key worker, trial, intent, consent) clear as soon as their field is filled, at save or later, as `update_communication` does now. Extend it to the new communication fields (`inform_by_date`, `gp_informed_date`).
7. Versions: every correction after save makes a new version holding the changed keys (key, old value, new value), who, when and the reason. Today only the decision text is in the version row. Add a full snapshot of the form fields to each version. An edit shows "edited by <name> on DD/MM/YYYY" on the record and in the Case File. A correction to the decision text, intent, divergent views or stage needs a reason; the others do not. The original is never overwritten. Confirmations by a clinician beat model output and survive re-process (existing rule).
8. Only the owner's consultants (and the coordinator within the meeting day) may edit; every edit is audited; the owner is told of a change to the decision, as with a new outcome.
9. Stage: `stage_as_agreed` must show its source. A Vaidyx draft stage is never saved unless a doctor has confirmed it (existing staging rule); the field never fills itself with a draft.
10. Divergent views: each row needs a named person and text; the card concerns are merged as now.
11. Dates in ISO in storage, DD/MM/YYYY on screen; reject a date earlier than the meeting for `patient_informed_date`'s later entry unless a reason is given.
12. Attendance and AI agents: an AI agent never counts as a member (existing rule); its answers are not recorded as the board's decision.
13. Show the same fields everywhere: minutes, deck, Case File "Board decisions" (item 3.2), FHIR and the NCG VTB export all read from one field list, so a new field appears in all.
14. Required fields in protocols (`post_board.required_record_fields`) stay, but the backend must read them for the new keys too; non-breast protocols were not checked.

## 7. Open questions for the owner

1. Intent: choice only (curative, palliative, not stated), or also "neoadjuvant, adjuvant, supportive" as extra values? The sources say only "intention to treat".
2. May `stage_as_agreed` be filled from a confirmed draft stage, or only from a stage written in a report?
3. Which referrals must the form always offer (the six you listed plus physiotherapy and social work)? Who in the department receives each?
4. PM-JAY: is "approval" the hospital's internal clinical approval, the package pre-authorization raised in the portal, or both? The manual does not define the record, so which reference (number, date) should be stored, if any?
5. Time rules: should "tell the patient by" default to 2 working days (Greater Manchester) or to the 7 days Vaidyx uses today for its dashboard? Should a GP date be asked at all in India (a GP letter may not apply)?
6. Is a noted case a decision for the dashboard and the metrics, or separate?
7. Who may correct after the meeting: only the owner's consultants, or also the MDT Coordinator for typing slips? Should a correction need a second person?
8. Should the original coordinator entry show beside the corrected one on the Case File (as proposed), or only on request?
9. Trial: Vaidyx has no trial list, so should it ask yes, no, not stated only, and a free-text trial name?
10. ACS CoC 2.5 and the ESMO paper could not be read in full from here. May a clinician check those two pages and confirm the wording before we cite them in the product?
11. India: no public NCG VTB rule on outcome recording and no public Indian hospital MDT policy was found in this pass. If the owner has the NCG VTB operating procedure or a hospital template (for example Tata Memorial), adding it would settle fields such as "board date, chair, centre of the patient" which the NCG template lists only as inputs.
